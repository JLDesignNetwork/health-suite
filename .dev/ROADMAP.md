## Strategic Vision

**Health Suite** is an all-in-one, local-first, zero-cloud personal and household health, nutrition, and medical record management platform. It combines structured biometric logging, trend analytics, Personal Health Records (PHR), a pantry stock manager, profile-tailored AI recipe generation, and a local AI health advisor.

## Generational Backlogs & Horizons

### Generation 2606 (Active Baseline)
- [x] **Household Profile Switching:** Apple TV–style multi-user profile picker with database isolation.
- [x] **Structured Biometric & Vitals Logging:** Daily weights, vitals, measurements, water, and exercise.
- [x] **Comprehensive PHR Module:** Allergies, conditions, surgeries, family history, immunizations, medications with AI interaction lookups.
- [x] **Kitchen & Pantry Inventory:** Stock counts (`quantity_on_hand`), live filtering, and automated stock reduction on meal consumption.
- [x] **Profile-Tailored AI Recipe Engine:** AI recipe generation factoring in exact pantry stock, medical conditions, allergies, and caloric targets.
- [x] **Local AI Health Advisor:** Contextual health consultations using local baselines and records.

### Generation 2608 (Multimodal & Input Expansion)
- [ ] **🎙️ Voice Dictation & Audio Entry:** Hands-free speech-to-text logging for daily vitals, symptoms, and meal entries.
- [ ] **📸 Grocery Receipt & Pantry Photo Scanning (OCR):** Computer vision / multimodal AI to automatically ingest pantry stock from grocery receipts or shelf photos.
- [ ] **📝 Clinical Notes & Narrative Logs:** Freeform medical encounter notes and narrative symptom logs complementing structured forms.

### Future Horizons (Ecosystem & Hardware Integration)
- [ ] **🔄 Health Ecosystem Sync:** Bi-directional sync with Apple HealthKit, Garmin, Google Health Connect, and Bluetooth scale/blood pressure monitors.
- [ ] **📑 Standardized Medical Export:** Export full PHR in standardized FHIR / HL7 formats for healthcare providers.
